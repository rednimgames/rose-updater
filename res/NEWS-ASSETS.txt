Native launcher news assets

news-background.webp and news-logo.webp are the original ROSE launcher artwork:
https://www.roseonlinegame.com/images/launcher-bg.webp
https://www.roseonlinegame.com/images/launcher-logo.webp

ROSENews-Regular.ttf and ROSENews-Bold.ttf use the same Josefin Sans 2.001
outlines as the launcher website's 400/700 font faces:
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/josefin-sans@5.3.0/latin-400-normal.woff2
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/josefin-sans@5.3.0/latin-700-normal.woff2

The WOFF2 files were decompressed to TrueType with fontTools for FLTK's native
font loader. The converted family is named "ROSE News" to respect the reserved
font name. Font outlines and metrics are unchanged. The SIL Open Font License
is embedded in each font's name table and reproduced in JosefinSans-OFL.txt.
